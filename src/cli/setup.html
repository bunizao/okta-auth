How to get a TOTP authenticator Okta accepts

okta-auth answers Google Authenticator or Okta Verify's code, never a push.

In Okta, open Settings → Security Methods and set up Google Authenticator. With 1Password, open your Okta login, add a One-Time Password field and scan the QR code into it; otherwise choose Can't scan? and copy the secret. Finish enrolling with a code, then come back here.

2 Try a sign-in

A page that sends you to Okta when signed out, or an Okta app link. One attempt only: a wrong password stops here, since retries lock the account.
